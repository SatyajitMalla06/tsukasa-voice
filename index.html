Commands

Ready to Drop

Filter by category or browse all commands below.

/play [song]
Search and play any track instantly
/pause
Pause the current track
/resume
Resume paused playback
/skip
Skip to the next track
/stop
Stop music and clear queue
/nowplaying
See what's currently playing
/seek [time]
Jump to a specific timestamp
/replay
Restart the current song
/volume [1–100]
Set volume to a specific level
/volume up
Increase volume by 10%
/volume down
Decrease volume by 10%
/volume max
Crank the volume to 100%
/mute
Mute without stopping playback
/unmute
Restore volume after muting
/queue
View the current playlist
/shuffle
Shuffle the queue randomly
/loop [off/track/queue]
Loop song or entire queue
/remove [position]
Remove a song by position
/move [from] [to]
Move a song to different position
/clearqueue
Wipe the entire queue clean
/filter bassboost
Deep bass enhancement effect
/filter nightcore
Faster tempo + pitched-up sound
/filter vaporwave
Slowed + reverbed vibes
/filter 8d
Spatial audio 8D surround effect
/filter karaoke
Remove vocals from the track
/filter off
Disable all active filters
/spotify connect
Link your Spotify account to the bot
/spotify playlist [name]
Queue a saved Spotify playlist
/spotify liked
Queue your Spotify liked songs
/spotify disconnect
Unlink your Spotify account
/join
Pull Tsukasa into your voice channel
/leave
Disconnect from voice channel
/djmode [on/off]
Lock controls to DJ role only
/prefix [char]
Change the bot command prefix
